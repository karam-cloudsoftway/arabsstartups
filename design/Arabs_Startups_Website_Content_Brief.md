## 4. Core editorial pillars

1. **Startup News:** Launches, product milestones, relevant ecosystem announcements, partnerships, funding, competitions, and developments. Verify facts and attribute sources.
2. **Founder Stories:** Conversations about what founders built, why they built it, decisions they made, and what they learned.
3. **Lessons From Failure:** Honest, respectful interviews about setbacks, pivots, failed experiments, and recovery. Never use failure as sensational content.
4. **Women in Entrepreneurship:** Interviews, insights, and coverage showcasing women founders and ecosystem leaders without tokenism.
5. **Webinars and Interviews:** Live sessions and recorded conversations with founders, operators, investors, mentors, and ecosystem builders.
6. **Podcasts and Video:** Recorded conversations and explainers, introduced progressively as production capacity grows.
7. **Ecosystem Guide (future):** Structured profiles of startups, investors, accelerators, incubators, universities, events, and other supporting organizations.
8. **Opportunities (optional):** Relevant startup jobs, open calls, accelerator deadlines, grants, events, and competitions. Date-stamp and verify every listing.

## 7. Editorial voice and writing rules

- Prioritize clear Modern Standard Arabic with approachable phrasing; avoid formal bureaucratic language and sensationalist headlines.
- Maintain correct Arabic RTL behavior, punctuation, and typography. Proper names and technical terms may appear in English when clearer.
- Write for founders, operators, students, ecosystem partners, and interested readers — not only venture capital professionals.
- Avoid political coverage; focus on entrepreneurship, business, innovation, and related economic developments.
- Verify dates, figures, investors, investment rounds, and participant names with credible primary or independently confirmed sources.
- Do not simply copy/rewrite other publishers' full articles. Produce original value, summarize facts fairly, credit sources, and respect licenses and copyright.
- Mark opinion, partner content, sponsored content, and press releases so readers can distinguish them from independent editorial reporting.
- Keep coverage inclusive of different startup communities and organizations, even if they compete with any investor, incubator, or accelerator associated with the brand.
- Publish corrections transparently, recording significant changes and their dates.
- Obtain consent for recorded interviews and appropriate rights to photos, audio, and other media.

- **Content blocks:** Lead story, featured cards, category chips, guest headshots with permission, interview/video cards, simple newsletter/contact CTA.

- **Images:** Use licensed editorial photography or supplied media. Avoid misleading synthetic "news photography" presented as a real event.
